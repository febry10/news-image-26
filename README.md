A 1024×1024 newspaper poster, "The World Today — Thursday, 26 November 2026", summing up the day's world news in a single image. The lead story marks one year since the Wang Fuk Court fire in Hong Kong that killed 168 people, with the independent report's finding that the blaze was preventable. Inside: a Jerusalem judge urging prosecutors to drop the Netanyahu bribery case, 31 killed in a tanker fire on Cairo's Ring Road, US stocks sliding on AI worries, North Korea expelling the Swiss envoy, Gukesh vs Sindarov for the World Chess Championship in Geneva, and briefs on Ukraine, the Houthi claim on Ras Tanura, TrumpCare and Modi's Nepal package. The poster is rendered from poster.html with headless Chromium; main.ts validates the PNG and copies it to the output directory as entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
