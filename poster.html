One Year On, Hong Kong Mourns 168 Lost in the Fire

A year after flames tore through Wang Fuk Court — the city’s deadliest fire since 1948 — Hong Kong marked the anniversary of the blaze that killed 168 people and displaced nearly 2,000 families. A final independent report says it was preventable: a lit cigarette, fire-safety failures and alleged fraud in the renovation wrapping seven of the eight 31-storey blocks in scaffolding and netting. It raced at about 10 metres a second.

Israel · Jerusalem

Judge Urges Prosecutors to Drop Netanyahu Bribery Case

A Jerusalem District Court judge told the attorney general the chances of conviction in the bribery case against prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu — Case 4000 — are low and the case should be dropped. Prosecutors were urged to reconsider Case 2000 and seek a deal in Case 1000. The advice is not a ruling. Netanyahu, who denies wrongdoing, demanded the trial end “immediately”.

Egypt · Cairo

31 Killed as Tanker Fire Engulfs Cairo Ring Road

A tanker carrying petroleum products overturned in Giza’s Al-Warraq district, spilling fuel that set more than a dozen vehicles ablaze on the capital’s ring road. Egypt’s health ministry put the toll at 31 dead and 21 injured, and warned the count was preliminary as firefighters cleared the charred wreckage above a tunnel in the densely packed neighbourhood.

Asia · Pyongyang

North Korea Expels Swiss Envoy in Sanctions Row

Pyongyang declared the head of Switzerland’s diplomatic office, Sylvain Astier, persona non grata and ordered him out within 48 hours, shutting an office Bern had reopened only in October. The move answers Swiss sanctions on the Songdowon children’s camp.
