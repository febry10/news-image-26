One Year On, Hong Kong Counts the Cost of Its Deadliest Fire

Hong Kong today marks one year since the Wang Fuk Court fire in Tai Po killed 168 people and displaced some 2,000 families — the city’s deadliest blaze since 1948. The official report calls it preventable: a lit cigarette, failed fire-safety systems, alleged fraud, and flammable scaffolding and netting wrapped around seven of the estate’s eight 31-storey blocks.
