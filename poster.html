Anniversary · Tai Po, Hong Kong

One Year On, Hong Kong Mourns 168 Lost in the Tai Po Fire

A year after flames tore through Wang Fuk Court — the city’s deadliest fire since 1948 — survivors returned to the towers to mark the anniversary. A final independent report says the blaze that killed 168 people and displaced nearly 2,000 families was preventable: a lit cigarette, fire-safety failures and alleged fraud in the renovation wrapping seven of the eight 31-storey blocks in scaffolding and netting. It raced at about 10 metres a second.

Israel · Jerusalem

Judges Urge Prosecutors to Drop Netanyahu Bribery Case

Judges at the Jerusalem District Court told the attorney general the chances of conviction in Case 4000 — the bribery case against prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu — are low and the case should be dropped. They urged prosecutors to reconsider Case 2000 and seek a deal in Case 1000. The advice is not a verdict. Netanyahu, who denies wrongdoing, demanded the trial end “immediately”.

Egypt · Cairo

31 Killed as Tanker Fire Engulfs Cairo Ring Road

A tanker carrying petroleum products overturned in Giza’s Al-Warraq district, spilling fuel that set more than two dozen vehicles ablaze on the capital’s ring road. Egypt’s health ministry put the toll at 31 dead and 21 injured, and warned the count was preliminary as firefighters cleared the charred wreckage above a tunnel in the densely packed neighbourhood.

Markets · New York

AI Fears Deepen the Sell-Off on Wall Street

US stocks fell further as fears over artificial intelligence intensified, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq down almost 4% in early trading and all three main indexes lower. The slide extends a rout in chip and software stocks as investors question AI spending.

Asia · Pyongyang

North Korea Expels Swiss Envoy in Sanctions Row

Pyongyang declared the head of Switzerland’s diplomatic office, Sylvain Astier, persona non grata and ordered him out within 48 hours, shutting an office Bern had reopened only in October. The move answers Swiss sanctions on the Songdowon children’s camp.
